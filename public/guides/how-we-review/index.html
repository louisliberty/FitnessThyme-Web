How we write guides & our disclosure

By the FitnessThyme editorial team · Updated November 2, 2026

Who writes these

Guides are published under the FitnessThyme editorial team. We aren’t doctors, dietitians or certified trainers writing in a professional capacity, and nothing here is medical advice.

How we research

Our guides are based on published sources, which we link at the bottom of every page. We say when a source is a company describing its own product, and we do not describe a product as “tested” unless we have tested it ourselves. At the moment we have not hands-on tested the services we write about. If that changes, we will say exactly what we did.

Prices and accuracy

Prices, plans and features change often. We note when information was gathered, but you should confirm current details with the provider. If you spot an error, tell us once our contact address is live on the contact page.

Affiliate disclosure

FitnessThyme may in future join affiliate programs, which means we could earn a commission if you buy through certain links. At the time of writing, our guides contain no affiliate links. If we add any, we will label them clearly near the link and on the page, and commissions will not change what we write about a product.

Health claims

We don’t make claims that a product treats, cures or prevents any condition. For any health concern, talk to a qualified professional.
