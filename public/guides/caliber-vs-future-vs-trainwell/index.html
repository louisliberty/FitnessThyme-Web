Caliber vs. Future vs. Trainwell: How They Differ

By the FitnessThyme editorial team · Published November 2, 2026

How this guide was made. It is based on the published sources listed at the bottom. The FitnessThyme editorial team has not personally tested these services, and prices and plans change, so confirm current details with each provider before you pay.

Caliber, Future and Trainwell are three of the better-known online coaching options. This comparison relies on published information only. Some of it comes from Trainwell itself, which is a seller, so we mark which claims are self-reported. We have not tested these services, and details change often.

Reading the table

  • Caliber is the only one with a free tier, so it is the lowest-risk way to see whether a strength-focused app suits you. Its human coaching is a separate, pricier product.
  • Future is the priciest per month in these sources, with a discounted first month. Work out the cost from month two.
  • Trainwell sits in between on price, but its description comes from its own marketing. Verify its trainer standards and rating claims yourself.

What none of this tells you

Whether a particular coach is a good fit for you. Whichever you consider, use the questions in our selection checklist, ask about cancellation, and test response time during any trial. Price and plan details may differ from what is listed here, so check each provider’s own site.
