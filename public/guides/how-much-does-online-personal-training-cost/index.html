How Much Does Online Personal Training Cost?

By the FitnessThyme editorial team · Published November 2, 2026

Disclosure: this page contains no affiliate links today. If we add any, they will be labeled and may earn FitnessThyme a commission. Read more.

How this guide was made. It is based on the published sources listed at the bottom. The FitnessThyme editorial team has not personally tested these services, and prices and plans change, so confirm current details with each provider before you pay.

Prices for online training vary widely because the products differ. A $15 app and a $199 coaching plan are not the same thing. Here is what recent published sources report. These figures were gathered in autumn 2026 and may have changed, so check each provider’s site.

What explains the price gap

  • A person versus software. The lowest-priced options rely on AI or pre-built classes. The $149–$199/month plans include a human coach who adjusts your program.
  • Billing structure. Some plans bill several months up front; others use a low introductory price. Compare the total over three months, not just the headline number.
  • Scope. Some coaching includes nutrition guidance; check what is and isn’t included.

Online coaching versus in-person

One source estimates that training in person three times a week at $75–$150 a session would run about $900 to $1,800 a month, against $149–$199 a month for the online human-coach plans above.[3] That comparison comes from a company that sells online coaching, and in-person pricing varies by city, so use it as a rough guide. Online coaching can cost less, but you won’t get a coach standing next to you to correct form in person.

Before you pay

  • Ask what the price is after any introductory offer.
  • Ask about the billing period and how to cancel.
  • Check whether the plan can be paid with an HSA or FSA account (eligibility depends on your plan and documentation; ask your plan administrator).
