10 Questions to Ask Before Hiring an Online Coach

By the FitnessThyme editorial team · Published November 2, 2026

Disclosure: this page contains no affiliate links today. If we add any, they will be labeled and may earn FitnessThyme a commission. Read more.

How this guide was made. It is based on the published sources listed at the bottom. The FitnessThyme editorial team has not personally tested these services, and prices and plans change, so confirm current details with each provider before you pay.

Copy these into an email or chat with any coach or company you are considering. Clear answers are a good sign. Vague ones are worth noticing.

  1. What are your qualifications and experience? Ask about certifications, education and how long you have coached online.
  2. What are the hiring standards for your coaches? If it’s a company, ask about the whole roster, not just the featured coaches.
  3. Who will I actually talk to? A named coach, or a support team?
  4. How do we communicate and how fast do you reply? Text, video, calls; typical response time.
  5. Do you reach out to me, or do I have to start every check-in?
  6. How do you adjust my program? Based on my workout data and feedback, or only at scheduled reviews?
  7. How do you handle injuries, travel and equipment limits?
  8. What is the total monthly cost? Including the billing period, any intro pricing and what is not included.
  9. How do I cancel, and is there a trial or refund policy? Ask where in the app or contract the cancellation steps live.
  10. Can I switch coaches, and is there a penalty?

These draw on the selection criteria in a published buyer’s guide.[1] The guide’s author sells online coaching; the questions are general, but treat its rankings with caution.

Red flags

  • Promises of specific results or guaranteed outcomes.
  • Pressure to sign up today, or a trial that is hard to cancel.
  • No clear answer on price or cancellation.
  • Advice that overrides what your doctor told you.
