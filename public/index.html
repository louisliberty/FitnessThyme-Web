About Maya

I'm a NASM-certified personal trainer with a specialty in prenatal and postpartum fitness. I've helped over 200 women stay strong and confident through pregnancy and return to full strength after birth — in a way that feels empowering, not overwhelming.

My approach blends evidence-based strength training with mobility work and breathwork. I'm passionate about making every client feel heard and understood — not pushed through a cookie-cutter program.
